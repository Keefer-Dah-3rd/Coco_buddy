# Coco-2 design migration

Bringing the redesigned Blue Skies Plus look from the Coco-2 Figma file
(`3y1ZhdqAPVIYPJf2bTuOsY`, page “01 - Coco Screens”) into the app. This is the working
checklist: tick items as they land and are checked against Figma in a snapshot or the running app.

## What changed in Figma

The screens keep their structure; the system underneath them moved:

- **Type:** every Sky text style is now Owners or Owners Wide (MCKL), with Inter (the system
  face in code) kept for a few numerals and titles.
- **Colour:** muted `#71777A → #595E60`, accent text `#D9480F → #F73C3C`, and new `confirm`
  `#51E302` (done, wins, switches), `blue2` navy `#0D0640` (rims, selected tab, dark cards),
  `bg2` indigo `#3B43BD` (tab bar), plus the receipt colours and `dusk-disc`.
- **Edges:** accent buttons, segments, chips and the wins card get 1 pt black/ink rims; inputs
  get an orange rim; panels and windows get a 2 pt navy rim.
- **Panel chrome:** the header is just the date (Owners Wide) and three plain round keys —
  Coco's name, status dot and speech line are gone; the tab bar is indigo with a navy
  selected tile; the panel is 620 pt tall (was 540) — now 700 (see Decisions).

## Decisions (asked 2026-10-27)

- Speech line: follow Figma and drop it. While the panel is open Coco only mouths along.
- Panel height: 620, tokens updated in Figma and code. Raised to 700 on 2026-11-04 (below).
- Panel width (asked 2026-10-28): 390. `panel/width` and `size/panel-width` are 390 in Figma and
  code, and the Figma panel screens, task screens and panel-wide library parts (header, tab bar,
  task row, consent card, create-task panel, task lists) now take their width from
  `size/panel-width`. The task form is 350 (panel minus the 20 pt gutters). Prototype frames moved
  the panel 50 pt left and up so it ends at Coco's feet, with every hotspot re-placed on its layer.
  Not resized: the task-motion demo frames on “02 - Mascot Motion”.
- Panel size (asked 2026-11-04): 425 × 700 (35 wider, 80 taller). Set in `design/tokens.json`
  (`panel/width`, `panel/height`, `size/panel-width`, `size/panel-height`) and the same four
  variables in Figma. Every panel screen, task screen and `Companion/Panel` variant now takes its
  height from `size/panel-height` as well as its width from `size/panel-width` (the task screens
  were still 540 tall; they're 700 like the app now). The task form is 385 (panel minus the 20 pt
  gutters). Banner cards in the screens are scaled to fill the 381 pt column (381 × 181), the way
  the app scales the banner with its card. Prototype frames keep each panel's bottom-right corner
  where it was beside Coco (panel at 699, 64; task screens at 699, 56), and all 113 hotspots were
  re-placed on their layers by name — many were already off their buttons after the banner
  landed. The Tasks section on “01 - Coco Screens” was re-spaced (rows 800 apart) so the taller
  frames don't overlap.
- Owners: used wherever it's installed (Adobe Fonts on the designer's Mac); the system face
  stands in elsewhere — SF Pro at the same size/weight, expanded width for Owners Wide. Adobe
  Fonts can't be bundled; an app licence from MCKL would let us ship the files.

## Unresolved differences

Platform and data:
- Without Owners installed the app shows SF Pro (expanded for Owners Wide); text then runs a
  little wider and some lines wrap differently.
- Inter roles use SF Pro, whose line height is a point taller at 11–13 pt; cards end up 1–4 pt
  taller than Figma. The welcome window is 624 pt (Figma 610) so step 3's last card isn't clipped.
- Switches are the macOS mini switch (a few points taller than Figma's).
- Screens show the user's real tasks, events and dates; Figma shows sample content.

Kept on purpose (real features Figma doesn't draw):
- Focus: the “During focus” Full/Small/Quiet row, the notifications note, the meeting heads-up;
  paused digits dim to 60%.
- Today: help rows under the empty state; the editor's “Under” row and five priority steps;
  overdue due dates stay red (Figma shows due dates in ink and has no overdue state).
- Week view: previous/next week arrows and load-based day bars.
- Connections: the reconnect/setup copy that has no Figma frame.

Design changes followed from Figma:
- The speech line, Coco's name and the status dot are gone from the panel header.
- The wins card lost its note; the focus banner lost its sun disc; done titles aren't struck through.
- The editor's due date is a chip that opens a month calendar (the typed date field is gone).

Snapshot-only artefacts (not in the app): rimmed shapes rendered with `cacheDisplay` show small
vertical ticks at their ends, placeholders render dark, switches render grey (inactive window),
and colours shift slightly with the display profile.
