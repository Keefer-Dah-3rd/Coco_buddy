Desktop Buddy · Coco

Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Desktop Buddy is a Mac app with a desktop companion named Coco. It helps you plan your day, run a focus timer and, if you choose, chat with an AI assistant and see your calendars. This policy explains what the app does with your information. It's written to match what the app actually does; if the two ever differ, the app is wrong and we'll fix it.

In short
  • No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. We don't run servers that receive your data, and we never sell it.
  • Your data stays on your Mac unless you connect a service or use the chat.
  • The chat sends data to Anthropic only after you allow it, and only what it needs to answer you.
  • You can delete everything at any time: Settings › Delete all my data.

Who we are

Desktop Buddy is made by John Keefe ("we"). Contact: johnkeefedesign@gmail.com.

What stays on your Mac

Everything the app keeps is stored on your Mac, inside the app's private storage area or your Keychain:

  • Your tasks, focus history and timer, saved as files in the app's container.
  • Your settings, such as where Coco sits and your workday hours.
  • A short-lived copy of calendar events (about a week back to a few weeks ahead), so your schedule shows quickly and offline.
  • Keys and sign-ins (your Claude API key, and your Google Calendar and Partiful calendar addresses), kept in the macOS Keychain.
  • Chats are kept in memory only and are gone when you quit the app.

We have no access to any of it.

What leaves your Mac, and where it goes

Data leaves your Mac only to talk to services you choose to use. The app connects directly to each one; nothing passes through servers of ours.

The chat (Anthropic)

The chat uses Claude, made by Anthropic, through an API key you supply. Before anything is sent, the app shows you exactly what will be shared and asks for your permission. If you allow it:

  • Sent with every message: what you type, or say in the chat.
  • Sent only when Coco needs it to answer:
    • your tasks, with their notes, priorities and due dates;
    • your focus timer and how long you’ve focused today;
    • calendar events from Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and Partiful (titles, times and places), and a guest's name only when you ask about meetings with that person;
    • Partiful invitations you paste in;
    • short notes about what you just did, such as confirming a task the chat suggested.
    The chat shows each time Coco looks something up.
  • Never sent: voice recordings, event descriptions, guest lists, or your sign-ins and keys for other services.

Anthropic handles this data under its own privacy policy and the terms of your Anthropic account, and usage is billed to that account. You can withdraw permission at any time in Settings › Privacy & data, which also stops the chat and clears the conversation.

Reporting a reply and contacting us

Under each chat reply there's a Report link, and Settings has Contact support. Each opens a new email in your own mail app, addressed to johnkeefedesign@gmail.com. A report email quotes the reply you're reporting; both add the app and macOS version. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. We use what you send only to look into the report or answer you, and delete it when we're done.

Google Calendar (optional)

  • How it connects: you paste your calendar's secret address in iCal format, copied from Google Calendar's settings. There's no Google sign-in, and the app gets no access to your Google account.
  • What it reads: the app downloads that calendar's events from Google about every half hour, keeps about the last week and the next six months on your Mac, and shows them in Coco's calendar. Event descriptions stay on your Mac.
  • What it changes: nothing. The address is read-only, so Coco can't add, move or delete Google Calendar events.
  • Keeping it private: the address is kept in your Keychain. Anyone who has it can see that calendar's events, so you can reset it in Google Calendar's settings at any time; then paste the new one.
  • Disconnecting: disconnecting forgets the address and deletes the cached events.

Apple Calendar (optional)

If you allow it, the app reads the calendars in the macOS Calendar app on your Mac. This is read-only: it never adds, changes or deletes those events. Event descriptions and guest names stay on your Mac. You can turn access off in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars.

Partiful (optional)

  • Invitations: when you paste a Partiful invitation into the chat, the app fetches that one public page from partiful.com to read the event's details. It sends no cookies or sign-in, and never fetches other sites.
  • Your Partiful calendar: if you add your personal Partiful calendar link, the app reads it about every half hour to show your Partiful events. The link is kept in your Keychain and the events are cached on your Mac.
  • Writing: nothing is ever written to Partiful.

Voice

When you press the microphone, macOS speech recognition turns what you say into text. This happens on your Mac whenever your language supports it; otherwise Apple processes it under Apple's privacy policy. The app never records or stores audio, and uses the transcript once.

Notifications

Focus-timer and meeting reminders are scheduled locally on your Mac.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of your data, to anyone.
  • No accounts with us, and no servers of ours that receive your data.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete all my data removes your tasks, focus history and timer, your calendar addresses and cached events (Google Calendar and Partiful), your Claude key and chat, your settings and your chat permission. Disconnecting any single service removes just that service's data. Deleting the app also removes its stored files.

Data already sent to Anthropic is held under its own policies; contact Anthropic to delete it there.

Children

Desktop Buddy isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. Since we don't collect personal information at all, there is nothing for us to hold or delete.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can see, export or delete everything the app holds, because it's on your own Mac. For data held by a connected service, use that service's controls. For questions about this policy, email johnkeefedesign@gmail.com.

Changes

If the app starts sharing a new kind of data, we'll update this page and the app will ask your permission again before it does. The effective date above shows when this policy last changed.
