Getting started

Where is the app?

Desktop Buddy has no Dock icon. Coco sits on your desktop. Click her, or press ⌃⌥C from any app, to open her panel. Right-click her for the menu, which includes Settings and Quit.

Coco disappeared

Open the panel with ⌃⌥C, then right-click Coco › Reset Position. If she's hidden, Settings › Show Coco on the desktop brings her back.

The key tricks

  • Hold the right-hand Option key and click: Coco runs there.
  • Hold the right-hand Command key and click: you throw her ball.
  • Hold the right-hand Shift key and click: you toss her a treat.

These need no special permissions. You can switch each one off in Settings.

Chat

The chat asks for an API key

The chat uses Claude by Anthropic with your own API key. Create one in the Anthropic Console, then paste it in Connections › Claude. Usage is billed to your Anthropic account, and the chat shows an estimate. Everything else in the app works without a key.

"Before you chat"

The first time you open the chat, Coco lists exactly what it would send to Anthropic. Nothing is sent until you choose Allow. To turn it off later, go to Settings › Privacy & data; this also clears the conversation. More detail is in the privacy policy.

Calendars and connections

Apple Calendar

In Connections, choose Connect Apple Calendar and allow access when macOS asks. If you said no earlier, turn it on in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars. Accounts you've added to the Calendar app, including Google, iCloud and Exchange, show up in Coco.

Google Calendar

  1. On a computer, open Google Calendar's settings.
  2. Under Settings for my calendars, choose your calendar, then Integrate calendar.
  3. Copy the Secret address in iCal format and paste it in Connections › Google Calendar.

It's read-only. Coco shows your events and knows when you're busy, but can't add, move or delete them. Changes you make in Google can take a little while to show up.

The public address doesn't work unless the calendar is public.

Can't find the secret address? Some work or school accounts have it turned off by an administrator. In that case, add the account to the Mac's Calendar app and connect Apple Calendar instead. If an address stops working, you may have reset it in Google: copy the new one.

Partiful

  • One invitation: paste a partiful.com link into the chat.
  • All your events: on partiful.com (on a computer), open an event, click the calendar icon under the details, choose Google Calendar, then Copy Link. Paste that link in Connections › Partiful.

Invitations that are private or password-protected can't be read from their page. If they're in your Partiful calendar, Coco reads them from there.

"Needs reconnecting"

A service stopped accepting the saved sign-in or token, for example because it expired or was revoked. Open Connections and connect it again. Coco keeps showing the last data she had until you do.

Voice and sounds

Press the microphone and allow microphone and speech access when macOS asks. If you said no earlier, turn it on in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone, and in Speech Recognition. To silence Coco's barks, right-click her › Mute Sounds, or use Settings › Sound & voice.

Your data

Everything stays on your Mac unless you connect a service or use the chat. Settings › Delete all my data removes all of it. See the privacy policy.
